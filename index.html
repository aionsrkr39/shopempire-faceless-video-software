The Problem

Short-form Video Sells, but Making It is a Grind

Many people who want to promote products online get stuck before the first post goes live.

  • You don't want to appear on camera or record your own voice.
  • Editing software takes time to learn, and each video takes hours.
  • Finding products worth promoting means jumping between marketplaces.
  • Posting the same video to several platforms is repetitive and easy to let slide.

The result?

Ideas pile up, posting is inconsistent, and the content never gets made. The seller built ShopEmpire to remove the camera, the editing skills and the manual publishing from that process.

FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need to show my face or use my voice?

According to the seller, no. The product appears on screen, the voice-over is generated automatically and any brand character is an AI mascot.

Do I need to buy the products I promote?

The seller says no. You promote products as an affiliate, and the video is built from the seller's own product photo.

How would I get paid?

Through the affiliate program of whichever marketplace you promote, at its published rates. The seller notes that you need approval for the program you use. Details are on the official product page.

Is there a monthly fee?

The seller states the Bundle is a one-time payment with no recurring charges, though video generation uses credits. Confirm current terms on the official page.

How many credits are included?

The seller states 30,000 in total: 10,000 with the core app plus 20,000 from Credit Booster Pro. Top-ups are available at member pricing.

Can I use it for client work?

Yes, per the seller. A commercial licence is included, and the Agency Dashboard offers 10 client workspaces, white-label videos and client reporting.

Is the White Label Reseller licence included?

No. The seller states it is a separate offer, as is the 120K credit pack. The Bundle covers the core app and the four upgrades.

Can I really earn income with this?

No result is guaranteed. The seller says earnings depend on the products you choose, how consistently you post and your market, and their page carries an income disclaimer. Read it before purchasing.
